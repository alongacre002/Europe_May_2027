Book Now — Before You Go

  • Flights US → MUC on May 13, and MUC → US on May 22
  • Weissen Rössl lake-view rooms (May 16–18). They sell out early.
  • Neuschwanstein timed tickets for May 20
  • Anniversary dinner reservation for May 20
  • Two rooms at every stop: Salzburg, St. Wolfgang, Innsbruck, Garmisch, Munich
  • Hallstatt Salt Mine or Skywalk tickets (optional)

Car & Logistics

  • Rent at the MUC airport rental center and return there on May 21. It's the same location, so no one-way fee.
  • Reserve an automatic. For 4 adults and luggage, get a midsize SUV or a station wagon (Kombi).
  • Tell the rental company you're driving into Austria
  • Buy the digital Austrian vignette (10-day version) online before crossing
  • Munich: S8 or S1 from the airport to Marienplatz or the main train station, ~40 min

Mid-May Notes

  • Mostly mild days with cool nights and occasional rain. Bring layers and a rain jacket.
  • Bring warm layers for the summits: Zugspitze, Nordkette, Schafberg
  • Tiroler Zugspitzbahn is closed for maintenance Apr 5 – May 5, 2027, and runs daily from May 6
  • Eagle's Nest road opening depends on the season. Check closer to the date.

Confirm Closer to the Trip

  • 2027 season dates for the Schafberg railway, Königssee boats and Eagle's Nest
  • Hallstatt Salzwelten hours. It reopened in Sept 2026 after a year-long closure.
  • Hotel rates and availability for two rooms at each stop
